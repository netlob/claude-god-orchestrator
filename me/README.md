# Me

Who you are, for Claude. Most of this is created during onboarding.

- `profile.md` — your role, how you work, your Claude plan, how you like Claude to work with you
- `goals.md` — what you are going for
- `people.md` — who you work with and how
- `names.md` — correct spellings of artists, people and companies
- `sources/` — originals about you (interview answers, your dictations), dated, never edited
- `thinking/` — your thinking notes, in your own words

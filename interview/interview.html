Home base interview

Ten questions. Turn on voice dictation and talk freely; long and messy is good. Your answers save as you type, in this browser on this computer, so you can close this and come back. No internet needed.

1. Which Claude plan are you on?

In the Claude app this is under Settings. Pick the closest.

2. Who are you and what do you do?

Your role, the company, what a normal week looks like. What takes most of your time, and what you wish took less.

3. What is on your plate right now?

Every project, artist, campaign, venture or idea you are working on. For each one: where it stands and what is next. Say names slowly or spell them.

4. What do you make, and for whom?

The things you produce (pitch decks, campaigns, contracts, films, releases, reports) and who receives them. What does good look like?

5. Where does your information live, and which tools do you use?

Email, WhatsApp, Drive, Notion, spreadsheets, meeting transcripts, voice memos on your phone. Where do things get lost?

6. Who do you work with?

Artists, colleagues, managers, labels, agencies, clients. Spell the names. How do you work together, and who decides what?

7. How do you think and decide, and how should Claude work with you?

Should it push back, just do it, explain a lot or a little? What do you want to learn along the way?

8. How do you use AI today, and what frustrates you?

What works, what does not, what you have given up on.

9. The next ninety days

What should be different by then? And what would make this a clear win after one month?

10. Anything sensitive or off limits?

Things that should not be written down, people whose details should stay out, contracts that need care.
